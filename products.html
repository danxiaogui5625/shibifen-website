FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask before cooperation

Can you help with both formula and packaging?

Yes. OEM/ODM discussion can include product direction, bottle or jar choice, labels, boxes and gift set presentation.

Can I start with one product first?

Yes. Many buyers begin with one hero product or a small starter collection, then expand after sample review.

What information should I send for quotation?

Useful details include product type, target quantity, packaging idea, destination country, timeline and any formula direction.

Do you support private label skincare projects?
